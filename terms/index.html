Sortwick Terms of Use

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. The game

Sortwick is a single-player puzzle game. You can play it without an account. Progress is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own iCloud account. Game Center leaderboards and achievements are optional: they work only while you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, under Apple's terms.

2. Apple's terms apply

Downloads and purchases are also governed by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/) and the App Store terms.

3. Virtual items

Coins, boosters and other items have no cash value, cannot be exchanged for money and cannot be transferred. Purchased items are delivered right away. Where the law gives you a right to withdraw from a purchase (for example 14 days in the EU), you agree, when you confirm the purchase, that delivery starts at once. You then lose that right for the items delivered. Refunds are handled by Apple (Report a Problem).

4. Prices

Prices are shown in your App Store currency next to every Buy button, and Apple asks you to confirm before charging. Prices in coins also show about what they cost in your currency. You can set a daily spending limit in Settings.

5. Subscription (Harbor VIP)

Monthly, renews automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date; benefits continue until the end of the paid period. There is no free trial. Refunds are handled by Apple.

6. Season pass

A one-time purchase for the current 28-day season only. It does not renew.

7. Ads and Remove Ads

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Reward videos are always optional; a reward is given only for a video watched to the end, and videos are limited per day. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes the ads between levels on your Apple ID; optional reward videos stay available. Harbor VIP also removes the ads between levels while it is active. If no video is available, play goes on without it.

8. Random rewards

Nothing random is sold: no purchase gives album crates, cards or other random items. Free random rewards show their odds in the game.

9. Fair play

Changing the device clock to gain rewards is not supported; the game may ignore rewards claimed that way, and scores gained that way may be removed from the Game Center leaderboards.

10. Changes

We may update the game and these terms; the date above changes when we do.
