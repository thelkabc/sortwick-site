Sortwick gameplay trailer

A 28-second look at Sortwick, a goods sort puzzle for iPhone and iPad: a level 105 board with a COMBO x3, the Storm level 100, a level win with three stars, and one tap that restores the Market Stalls in Saltwick Harbor.
