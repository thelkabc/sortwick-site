What is Sortwick?

Sortwick is a free goods sort puzzle for iPhone and iPad. You move goods between shelves, match three of a kind to clear them, and spend the stars you win restoring Saltwick, a small seaside town. It is a cozy sorting game for adults who like a tidy shelf and a real challenge: triple match rules, hundreds of levels, playable offline.

How it plays in 10 seconds

Move goods between shelves and make sets of three to clear them. Every level is solvable, and every win earns stars you spend restoring Saltwick, one lighthouse, café and lantern at a time.

  1. 1Tap a good, tap a shelf

    Move one item at a time to any shelf with a free spot.

  2. 2Three of a kind clear

    Bring three identical goods together and they pop off the shelf.

  3. 3Win stars, restore Saltwick

    Every level you clear earns stars for the next place in town.

Questions

What is a goods sort game?

A goods sort game (also called goods sorting, triple match or match 3 goods) is a puzzle where you move items between shelves until three identical ones sit together and clear. Sortwick is a sorting game of this kind, with a seaside town you restore with the stars you win.

Is Sortwick free?

Yes. Sortwick is free to download and free to play. Purchases are optional and never change a level's solution, and reward videos are always your choice.

Does Sortwick work offline?

Yes. Levels, the town and your progress work with no internet and no account. Only extras such as leaderboards, purchases and reward videos need a connection.

Is there a sorting game without forced ads?

In Sortwick no ad interrupts the first 100 levels, and an ad never follows a loss. After that, ads between levels are rare by design, and one purchase (Remove Ads) removes them for good.

How is Sortwick different from other goods sorting games?

Levels are limited by moves instead of a countdown (only the occasional Rush level runs on a clock), and every level is solvable. On top of that there is a town with residents who talk back, a small download of about 25 MB, and offline play.

How do lives work?

A failed level costs one life; the first levels are free to retry. Lives refill on their own over time. When you run out, a free video gives one back, the Store sells a refill for coins, Harbor VIP adds an extra life, and the Starter Pack includes 1 hour of unlimited lives.

Are there ads?

None in the first levels. After that, reward videos are always your choice (an extra life, a few more moves, a free booster). An ad between levels is rare by design: none in the first 100 levels and never after a loss. Remove Ads (one purchase), Harbor VIP and any recent purchase turn those off; the optional videos stay. Right after level 1 the game asks for your ad consent (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) and whether it may use your advertising identifier; say no and ads are simply not personalised.

What can I buy?

Everything is optional. Coin packs of 500, 1,400, 3,100 and 8,500 coins; a one-time Starter Pack with 900 coins, 6 boosters and 1 hour of unlimited lives; Harbor VIP, a monthly subscription with 60 coins a day, a weekly chest, an extra life and no ads between levels; Harvest Pass Premium for the 28-day season; and Remove Ads. Prices appear in your App Store currency next to every Buy button, and Apple asks you to confirm before charging. Coins buy continues, lives and boosters; they never change a level's solution. You can set a daily spending limit in Settings.

How do I restore purchases or cancel Harbor VIP?

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you bought with. Harbor VIP renews monthly until you cancel it in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Does it use Game Center?

Yes, if you are signed in. Your Daily Challenge score and your weekly Sea Race wins go to two leaderboards, and 91 achievements track your progress. Signed out, nothing is sent and the game plays exactly the same; it never shows a sign-in screen of its own.

What data does the game collect?

No personal data: there is no account, and we run no servers that receive your data. The game sends anonymous gameplay statistics, with no personal data, so we can balance it; you can turn them off in Settings. Progress stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. Ads come from Google AdMob, which receives the usual ad data, personalised only with your permission. Game Center scores and purchases are handled by Apple. The details are in the privacy policy.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later; it also runs on iPad. In English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Japanese and Korean.
