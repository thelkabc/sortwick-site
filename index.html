What is in Sortwick

Move goods between shelves and make sets of three to clear them. Every level is solvable, and every win earns stars you spend restoring Saltwick, one lighthouse, café and lantern at a time.

New tricks, one at a time

  • Cracked shelves, chutes, carousels, counters, blizzards and order tickets join ice, locks, gifts, chains, crates, fog, rush levels and the belt
  • A Level Start sheet shows each new trick on the level's own goods before your first move, and the How-to-play guide keeps every rule one tap away
  • Tricks pair up, variants arrive, and double shifts send a second wave of goods onto the board

A town that talks back

  • Seven areas to restore, from Saltwick Harbor to the Harbor Lights Festival, then islands beyond
  • Residents like Nan, Basil, Clementine and Mara move in once their homes are fixed: they stroll the promenade, and a tap gets you a line
  • Every restore is a short conversation: the resident speaks, Pip the harbor gull answers
  • The bakery and the lighthouse do something: Basil's breakfast adds 2 moves to your first level of the day, and the lighthouse keeper pays your first Daily Challenge retry

A real challenge

  • Difficulty rises for real: HARD levels, SUPER HARD levels, boss levels, mystery levels and calm breathers
  • Boards come as towers, staircases, split columns, open shelves and more
  • Undo, Shuffle and Magnet boosters when you want them, never forced on you

Cozy and offline-friendly

  • Plays offline, no account needed
  • Daily gifts, quests, a Daily Challenge, a weekly Sea Race and a 28-day season
  • Collect cards in the album, unlock achievements and decorate Pip's house with furniture you collect
  • Free to play. Purchases never change a level's solution, and album crates can't be bought: their odds are always shown

Questions

How do lives work?

You have 3 lives. A failed level costs one from level 13 on (the first levels are free to retry), and a lost life comes back every 2 hours. The Store sells a refill for coins, Harbor VIP adds a 4th life, and the Starter Pack includes 1 hour of unlimited lives.

Are there ads?

Not before level 10. From then on, reward videos are always your choice (an extra life, a few more moves, a free booster), at most 10 a day. A short ad can play after some wins from level 15, at most 5 a day, never after a loss. Remove Ads (one purchase) and Harbor VIP turn those off; the optional videos stay. Right after level 1 the game asks for your ad consent (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) and whether it may use your advertising identifier; say no and ads are simply not personalised.

What can I buy?

Everything is optional. Coin packs of 500, 1,400, 3,100 and 8,500 coins; a one-time Starter Pack with 900 coins, 6 boosters and 1 hour of unlimited lives; Harbor VIP, a monthly subscription with 60 coins a day, a weekly chest, a 4th life and no ads between levels; Harvest Pass Premium for the 28-day season; and Remove Ads. Prices appear in your App Store currency next to every Buy button, and Apple asks you to confirm before charging. Coins buy continues, lives and boosters; they never change a level's solution. You can set a daily spending limit in Settings.

How do I restore purchases or cancel Harbor VIP?

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple account you bought with. Harbor VIP renews monthly until you cancel it in iOS Settings > your name > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Does it use Game Center?

Yes, if you are signed in. Your Daily Challenge score and your weekly Sea Race wins go to two leaderboards, and 91 achievements track your progress. Signed out, nothing is sent and the game plays exactly the same; it never shows a sign-in screen of its own.

What data does the game collect?

Nothing for us: no account, no analytics, no servers of ours. Progress stays on your iPhone (iCloud sync is not part of this version). Ads come from Google AdMob, which receives the usual ad data, personalised only with your permission. Game Center scores and purchases are handled by Apple. The details are in the privacy policy.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later; it also runs on iPad in iPhone compatibility mode. In English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Japanese and Korean.
