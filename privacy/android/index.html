Sortwick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

This policy is for Sortwick on Android; the iPhone and iPad version has its own policy at https://sortwick.com/privacy/. Sortwick is a puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics, with no personal data, so we can balance the game; you can turn them off in Settings. It shows ads from Google AdMob; in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland they are personalised only if you allow it, and elsewhere you can turn personalisation off in Android's ad settings. Your game progress stays on your device and, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, in your own Play Games account. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores and achievements go to Google Play Games; we do not receive personal data from it.

What the app stores

Game progress on your device: levels, coins, boosters, town progress, album and achievement progress, the profile name you choose (if you type one), settings and the purchase records the game needs (for example which store purchases were already delivered), plus simple ad counters (how many reward videos you watched today, when the last ad between levels was shown). This lives in the app's private storage on your Android device, is not included in Android's device backup, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage; a copy synced to Google Play Games stays in your Play Games account until you delete it there (see Saved games). Google's consent tool also stores your ad choices on your device.

No personal data is sent to us

We do not run servers that receive your data. The only third-party code in the app is Google's Mobile Ads SDK and its consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform), used to show ads; Google Play's libraries for purchases (Google Play Billing), Google Play Games and the in-app review prompt; and TelemetryDeck's SDK for the anonymous gameplay statistics described below. There are no attribution or crash-reporting libraries, and nothing in the app checks whether an ad led to the install. About once a day the app may download a small, signed game-balance settings file from our website (sortwick.com); this request sends nothing about you or your game, only what any internet request carries (such as your IP address). Our website host may keep that IP address in its access logs for a short time for security; we do not use it.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To make levels fair and the coin prices right, the app sends anonymous statistics about play to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), a privacy-focused analytics service. What is sent: which level you started and how it ended (won, lost or left, moves left, stars, boosters and continues used, roughly how long it took), when you ran out of lives or coins and what you did next (waited, watched a video, bought, or stopped), which store product a purchase was for (no prices, names or payment details), when an ad video or an ad between levels was shown, town restorations, tutorial steps, your answer to the ad consent question, coin spends in ranges, and rough figures such as days since you first played in ranges. TelemetryDeck's SDK adds technical details of the app and device to each statistic: app version and build, Android version, device brand and model, processor type, screen size, density and orientation, language, region, locale and time zone, light or dark mode, text direction, accessibility settings (such as font size, bold text, high-contrast text, colour inversion or removed animations), the day, week, month, year and hour, whether the app was installed from Google Play or from somewhere else, and simple counts it keeps on the device (the date of your first session, how many sessions and on how many days you played, and their average length). It also sends a statistic of its own when a session starts and once when it sees a new install. No personal data is sent: no name, email, Google account, advertising ID or location, and TelemetryDeck does not store your IP address. Instead of an identifier, TelemetryDeck receives a salted one-way hash of a random identifier the app creates for this installation (never the advertising ID, and not linked to your Google account), so statistics can be counted per player without anyone, including us, being able to tell who you are. The random identifier is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. The statistics are not linked to you and not used for tracking or ads. We see only totals in TelemetryDeck's dashboard. Statistics are sent in the background and never slow the game. You can turn them off any time in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous gameplay statistics; from then on nothing is sent, including statistics still waiting to be sent, which are deleted on your device. TelemetryDeck's privacy policy: https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/

Ads

Sortwick shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for a reward (for example 3 extra moves or a life), and a short ad between some levels after a win (at most 5 a day). There are no ads before level 10, none between levels in your first session, after a loss or for Harbor VIP members and Remove Ads owners. To show ads, limit how often you see them, count views and fight fraud, Google may collect: your device's advertising ID (where Google's consent message applies, only as your answer allows; you can reset or delete it in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads) and app set ID; your IP address (which gives an approximate location); device and app information such as the device model, Android version, language and app version; and how you interact with ads (views, taps, finished videos). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and explains how it uses data in ads at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data.

Consent and ad choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Elsewhere, ads may be personalised unless you turn that off: delete or reset your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads, and manage Google's ad personalisation at https://myadcenter.google.com. Android has no separate tracking prompt, so the app shows none. Ads are not set up as directed at children.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google through Google Play. We never see your name, email, card or payment details. The app receives a purchase record from Google Play (product, purchase time and a purchase token) only to deliver what you bought, to restore it and to check that a subscription is still active. Your saved game keeps the product, the time and a one-way hash of the purchase token, never the token itself.

Notifications

If you allow them, local notifications (for example "your lives are full") are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server. On Android 13 and later, Android asks for your permission first. After the device restarts, the app sets the reminders it had already planned again.

Google Play Games (optional)

Sortwick uses Google Play Games for two leaderboards (the Daily Challenge and the weekly Sea Race) and for achievements. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app sends your scores and achievement progress to Google Play Games after you play, and Google shows them under your Play Games name, to other players as your Play Games profile settings allow. Google processes this data under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). The app does not receive personal data from Google Play Games: it only reads your Play Games name and player ID to show your own row, and what Google Play Games shows to every player (names and scores on a leaderboard) to display the board; friends appear only if you have allowed Google Play Games to share your friends list with Sortwick, which the game never asks for. If you already use Google Play Games on this device, Google Play Games signs you in when the game starts, as it does for every game on Android; otherwise nothing is sent. You can sign in from the game (Settings, Profile, the Sea Race or a challenge result), which opens Google's own sign-in. The game never shows a sign-in screen on its own. You can manage your Play Games profile, its visibility and your connected games in the Google Play Games app or your Google account.

Saved games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the app keeps a copy of your game save in your own Play Games account (Google Play Games saved games) so your progress follows you to another Android device and survives a reinstall. The save is the game progress listed above, including your profile name, the purchase records the game needs (which products you bought and when, with a one-way hash of the purchase token; never payment details) and the sync bookkeeping that keeps coins from being counted twice, which includes a random identifier for this installation. It is stored by Google under its privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); we cannot read it and we receive nothing from it. Sync happens on launch, when the app returns to the foreground or goes to the background and after wins, only while the device is online; otherwise it simply waits. Saves of the iPhone and iPad version and of the Android version are separate and are never combined. To delete the copy, delete Sortwick's data from your Google Play Games profile (in the Google Play Games app or at https://play.google.com/games/profile); the game on your devices keeps its progress and uploads a new copy at its next sync while you stay signed in. Settings > Saved games shows the sync status.

Children

Sortwick is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data ourselves; ad data is handled by Google as described under Ads.

Your choices

You can delete all game data on the device by uninstalling the app or with Android Settings > Apps > Sortwick > Storage > Clear storage, and the Play Games copy as described under Saved games. Settings > Reset game progress starts the game over at level 1; it keeps the purchase records and today's spending-limit count (so nothing is delivered twice and the limit still holds), a few markers that stop one-time offers and daily rewards from being claimed twice, and your sound and motion settings. Resetting game progress does not change your ad choices (your consent answer and Android's advertising ID setting) or a Remove Ads purchase; change ad choices in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices and in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. You can turn anonymous gameplay statistics off in Settings > Privacy. You can turn notifications off in Settings or in Android Settings. You can set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings. Sortwick has no accounts, so there is no Sortwick account to delete; for any other request about your data, write to the address below.
