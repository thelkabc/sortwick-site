Sortwick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Summary

Sortwick is a puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own and no analytics. It shows ads from Google AdMob; they are personalised only if you allow it. Your game progress stays on your device. If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Game Center; we do not receive personal data from it.

What the app stores

Game progress on your device: levels, coins, boosters, town progress, album and achievement progress, settings and the purchase records the game needs (for example which store transactions were already delivered), plus simple ad counters (how many reward videos you watched today, when the last ad between levels was shown). This lives in the app's local storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app. Google's consent tool also stores your ad choices on your device.

Nothing is sent to us

We do not run servers that receive your data. The only third-party code in the app is Google's Mobile Ads SDK and its consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform), used to show ads. There are no analytics, attribution or crash-reporting libraries.

Ads

Sortwick shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for a reward (for example 3 extra moves or a life), and a short ad between some levels after a win (at most 5 a day). There are no ads before level 10, none between levels in your first session, after a loss or for Harbor VIP members and Remove Ads owners. To show ads, limit how often you see them, count views and fight fraud, Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking; your IP address (which gives an approximate location); device and app information such as the phone model, iOS version, language and app version; and how you interact with ads (views, taps, finished videos). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and explains how it uses data in ads at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data.

Consent and ad choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. After that, iOS asks whether Sortwick may track you; we ask only if your consent answer allows personalised ads. If you decline either, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change the tracking choice any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads are not set up as directed at children.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or payment details. The app receives a transaction record from Apple (product, date, and for the subscription its end date) only to deliver what you bought and to restore it.

Notifications

If you allow them, local notifications (for example "your lives are full") are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Game Center (optional)

Sortwick uses Apple's Game Center for two leaderboards (the Daily Challenge and the weekly Sea Race) and for achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the app sends your scores and achievement progress to Game Center after you play, and Game Center shows them under your Game Center nickname. Apple processes this data under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app does not receive personal data from Game Center: it only reads what Game Center shows to every player (nicknames and scores on a leaderboard) to display the board, and the Friends tab needs your separate permission, which Game Center asks for. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent and the leaderboards stay hidden. You can sign in or out in iOS Settings > Game Center. The app never shows a sign-in screen of its own.

iCloud save sync (not active yet)

This version does not sync your progress to iCloud. A future version may offer iCloud save sync; if you turn it on, your save will be stored in your own iCloud account under Apple's privacy policy, and we will not be able to read it. This policy will be updated before that feature ships.

Children

Sortwick is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data ourselves; ad data is handled by Google as described under Ads.

Your choices

You can delete all game data by deleting the app. Settings > Reset game progress starts the game over at level 1; it keeps the purchase records and today's spending-limit count (so nothing is delivered twice and the limit still holds), a few markers that stop one-time offers and daily rewards from being claimed twice, and your sound and motion settings. Resetting game progress does not change your ad choices (your consent answer and the iOS tracking setting) or a Remove Ads purchase; change ad choices in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices and in iOS Settings. You can turn notifications off in Settings. You can set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings.
