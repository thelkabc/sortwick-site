Sortwick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Summary

Sortwick is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device.

What the app stores

Game progress on your device: levels, coins, boosters, town progress, settings and the purchase records the game needs (for example which store transactions were already delivered). This lives in the app's local storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app.

Nothing is sent to us

We do not run servers that receive your data. The app contains no third-party SDKs: no ad, analytics, attribution or crash-reporting libraries.

Ads

This version shows no ads of any kind. If a future version ever adds ads, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated in that same version, before any ad is shown.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or payment details. The app receives a transaction record from Apple (product, date, and for the subscription its end date) only to deliver what you bought and to restore it.

Notifications

If you allow them, local notifications (for example "your lives are full") are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Optional Apple services (not active yet)

Future versions may offer Game Center (leaderboards, achievements) and iCloud save sync. If you turn these on, Apple processes that data under Apple's privacy policy; we would only receive what Game Center shows to every player (for example your Game Center nickname and score on a leaderboard). This policy will be updated before such features ship.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can delete all game data by deleting the app. You can turn notifications off in Settings. You can set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings.

Contact

Questions: privacy@sortwick.com
