Sortwick Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

Sortwick is a puzzle game. It has no accounts of its own. It sends anonymous gameplay statistics, with no personal data, so we can balance the game; you can turn them off in Settings. It shows ads from Google AdMob; they are personalised only if you allow it. Your game progress stays on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, in your own iCloud account. If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements go to Game Center; we do not receive personal data from it.

What the app stores

Game progress on your device: levels, coins, boosters, town progress, album and achievement progress, the profile name you choose (if you type one), settings and the purchase records the game needs (for example which store transactions were already delivered), plus simple ad counters (how many reward videos you watched today, when the last ad between levels was shown). This lives in the app's local storage on your iPhone and is deleted when you delete the app; a copy synced to iCloud stays in your iCloud account until you delete it there (see iCloud save sync). Google's consent tool also stores your ad choices on your device.

No personal data is sent to us

We do not run servers that receive your data. The only third-party code in the app is Google's Mobile Ads SDK and its consent tool (Google User Messaging Platform), used to show ads, and TelemetryDeck's SDK for the anonymous gameplay statistics described below. There are no attribution or crash-reporting libraries. About once a day the app may download a small game-balance settings record from Apple's CloudKit (the app's public database); this request sends nothing about you or your game, only what any internet request carries (such as your IP address), and Apple handles it under Apple's privacy policy. Our website (sortwick.com, where this policy and the Terms are published) is run by a web hosting provider; when you open it, the provider may keep technical access logs, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the site working, under its own privacy policy; we do not use them to identify you. Once after installing, the app asks Apple's AdServices whether the game was installed from an Apple Search Ads ad; the answer (ad or not, and the campaign number) stays on your device.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To make levels fair and the coin prices right, the app sends anonymous statistics about play to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany), a privacy-focused analytics service. What is sent: which level you started and how it ended (won, lost or left, moves left, stars, boosters and continues used, roughly how long it took), when you ran out of lives or coins and what you did next (waited, watched a video, bought, or stopped), which store product a purchase was for (no prices, names or payment details), when an ad video or an ad between levels was shown, town restorations, tutorial steps, your answer to the ad consent and tracking questions, coin spends in ranges, and rough figures such as days since you first played in ranges. TelemetryDeck's SDK adds technical details of the app and device to each statistic: app version and build, iOS version, device model, screen size and orientation, language, region and time zone, light or dark mode, accessibility settings (such as text size, Bold Text or Reduce Motion), the day of the week, month and year, whether the app came from the App Store or TestFlight, and simple counts it keeps on the device (the date of your first session, how many sessions and on how many days you played, and their average length). It also sends a statistic of its own when a session starts and once when it sees a new install. No personal data is sent: no name, email, Apple ID, advertising identifier or location, and TelemetryDeck does not store your IP address. Instead of an identifier, TelemetryDeck receives a salted one-way hash of the app's identifier for this device (Apple's identifier for vendor, never the advertising identifier), so statistics can be counted per player without anyone, including us, being able to tell who you are. The statistics are not linked to you and not used for tracking or ads. We see only totals in TelemetryDeck's dashboard. Statistics are sent in the background and never slow the game. You can turn them off any time in Settings > Privacy > Share anonymous gameplay statistics; from then on nothing is sent, including statistics still waiting to be sent, which are deleted on your device. TelemetryDeck's privacy policy: https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/

Ads

Sortwick shows ads from Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch for a reward (for example 3 extra moves or a life), and a short ad between some levels after a win (at most 5 a day). There are no ads before level 10, none between levels in your first session, after a loss or for Harbor VIP members and Remove Ads owners. To show ads, limit how often you see them, count views and fight fraud, Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking; your IP address (which gives an approximate location); device and app information such as the phone model, iOS version, language and app version; and how you interact with ads (views, taps, finished videos). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and explains how it uses data in ads at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not receive this data.

Consent and ad choices

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before any ad is requested, and you can change it any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. After that, iOS asks whether Sortwick may track you; we ask only if your consent answer allows personalised ads. If you decline either, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change the tracking choice any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads are not set up as directed at children.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, card or payment details. The app receives a transaction record from Apple (product, date, and for the subscription its end date) only to deliver what you bought and to restore it.

Notifications

If you allow them, local notifications (for example "your lives are full") are scheduled on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Game Center (optional)

Sortwick uses Apple's Game Center for two leaderboards (the Daily Challenge and the weekly Sea Race) and for achievements. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the app sends your scores and achievement progress to Game Center after you play, and Game Center shows them under your Game Center nickname. Apple processes this data under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The app does not receive personal data from Game Center: it only reads what Game Center shows to every player (nicknames and scores on a leaderboard) to display the board, and the Friends tab needs your separate permission, which Game Center asks for. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. You can sign in from the game (Settings, Profile, the Sea Race or a challenge result), which opens Apple's own Game Center sign-in, or in iOS Settings > Game Center, where you can also sign out. The game never asks you to sign in on its own.

iCloud save sync

If your iPhone is signed in to iCloud and iCloud is allowed for Sortwick, the app keeps a copy of your game save in your own iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit private database) so your progress follows you to another device and survives a reinstall. The save is the game progress listed above, including your profile name, the purchase records the game needs (which products you bought and when; never payment details) and the sync bookkeeping that keeps coins from being counted twice. It is stored under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we cannot read it and we receive nothing from it. Sync happens on launch, when the app returns to the foreground and after wins; without network or iCloud it simply waits. To turn it off, open iOS Settings > your name > iCloud, find Sortwick in the list of apps using iCloud (Saved to iCloud > See All; on older iOS versions Apps Using iCloud > Show All) and switch it off. To delete the copy, use iOS Settings > your name > iCloud > Manage Storage (on older iOS versions Manage Account Storage) > Sortwick > Delete; the game on your devices keeps its progress and uploads a new copy at its next sync unless sync is off. Settings > iCloud save shows the sync status.

Children

Sortwick is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not collect personal data ourselves; ad data is handled by Google as described under Ads.

Your choices

You can delete all game data on the device by deleting the app, and the iCloud copy as described under iCloud save sync. Settings > Reset game progress starts the game over at level 1; it keeps the purchase records and today's spending-limit count (so nothing is delivered twice and the limit still holds), a few markers that stop one-time offers and daily rewards from being claimed twice, and your sound and motion settings. Resetting game progress does not change your ad choices (your consent answer and the iOS tracking setting) or a Remove Ads purchase; change ad choices in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices and in iOS Settings. You can turn anonymous gameplay statistics off in Settings > Privacy. You can turn notifications off in Settings. You can set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings.
