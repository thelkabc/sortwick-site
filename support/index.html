Support

Need a hand? Write to support@sortwick.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use.

Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so sign in with the same one you bought with.

How do I reset my progress?

Your progress lives only on your device. To start over, delete the app and install it again; this removes your levels, coins and town progress. Please note this cannot be undone.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, choose Harbor VIP and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple (Report a Problem).

How do I limit spending?

Set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings.

How do I contact you?

Email support@sortwick.com. For privacy questions use privacy@sortwick.com.
