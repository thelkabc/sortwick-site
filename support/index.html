Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so sign in with the same one you bought with.

How do I reset my progress?

In the game open Settings and tap Reset game progress. The first screen lists exactly what you lose (levels, stars, town, coins, boosters, album, achievements, race and season progress) and what you keep; then hold the red button for 2 seconds or type RESET to confirm. You start again at level 1 like a new install. Harbor VIP, Remove Ads and season pass Premium stay yours (Restore Purchases brings them back if needed), and so do your ad choices; coins bought earlier are not returned. This cannot be undone.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, choose Harbor VIP and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Refunds are handled by Apple (Report a Problem).

How do I change my ad choices?

In the game open Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this opens Google's consent options; everywhere, iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking decides whether ads may be personalised. Resetting game progress does not change these choices.

Can I turn ads off?

Reward videos are always optional. Remove Ads (a one-time purchase in the Store) removes the ads between levels; Harbor VIP does too while it is active. Restore Purchases brings Remove Ads back on a new device.

How do I limit spending?

Set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings.

How do I contact you?

Email support@sortwick.com. For privacy questions use privacy@sortwick.com.
