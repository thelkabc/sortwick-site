Support

Need a hand? Write to support@sortwick.com. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and the game version (Settings, at the bottom).

Questions

How do lives work?

You have 3 lives. A failed level costs one from level 13 on, and a lost life comes back every 2 hours; the lives pill on Home shows the exact countdown. The Store sells a refill for coins, an optional video gives one back (up to 3 a day), Harbor VIP adds a 4th life, and the Starter Pack includes 1 hour of unlimited lives. If you allow notifications, the game tells you when your lives are full.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Store in the game and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple account, so sign in with the same one you bought with. Remove Ads, Harbor VIP and Harvest Pass Premium come back this way; coin packs and the Starter Pack are consumed when bought and are not restored.

How do I cancel Harbor VIP?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Harbor VIP and cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Benefits stay until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled by Apple (Report a Problem at reportaproblem.apple.com).

Can I turn ads off?

Reward videos are always optional and never play on their own. Remove Ads (a one-time purchase in the Store) removes the ads between levels; Harbor VIP does too while it is active. There are no ads of any kind before level 10, and the ads between levels are limited to 5 a day. Restore Purchases brings Remove Ads back on a new device.

How do I change my ad choices?

In the game open Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland this opens Google's consent options; everywhere, iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking decides whether ads may be personalised. Resetting game progress does not change these choices.

How does Game Center work here?

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (iOS Settings > Game Center), your Daily Challenge score and your weekly Sea Race wins are posted to the two leaderboards and your achievements are reported. The game never shows a sign-in screen of its own. Signed out, nothing is sent, the leaderboards stay hidden and the game plays the same. Achievements earned while signed out are reported once you sign in.

Is my progress backed up or synced?

Progress is saved on your iPhone and is included in your normal iPhone backup. This version does not sync progress between devices; deleting the app deletes the progress. Purchases can always be restored through the Store.

How do I reset my progress?

In the game open Settings and tap Reset game progress. The first screen lists exactly what you lose (levels, stars, town, coins, boosters, album, achievements, race and season progress) and what you keep; then hold the red button for 2 seconds or type RESET to confirm. You start again at level 1 like a new install. Harbor VIP, Remove Ads and Harvest Pass Premium stay yours (Restore Purchases brings them back if needed), and so do your ad choices; coins bought earlier are not returned. Achievements already reported to Game Center stay there. This cannot be undone.

How do I limit spending?

Set a daily spending limit in the game's Settings. Every price is shown in your App Store currency, and Apple asks you to confirm before charging.

Which devices and iOS versions?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. On iPad the game runs in iPhone compatibility mode. The game is in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Japanese and Korean, following your iPhone language.

How do I contact you?

Email support@sortwick.com. For privacy questions use privacy@sortwick.com. Press: see the press kit.
